LungFib scRNA-seq

Single-cell RNA sequencing analysis of innate immune cell states in lung fibrosis

Why this project?

Chronic lung injury and inflammation can lead to fibrosis through persistent activation of innate immune cells such as macrophages, neutrophils, and dendritic cells. While fibrosis-associated pathways are known at the tissue level, the cell-type–specific transcriptional programs driving pro-fibrotic immune states remain incompletely characterized.

Bulk transcriptomics averages signals across heterogeneous populations. Single-cell RNA sequencing resolves this heterogeneity and exposes disease-relevant cellular states.

This project uses scRNA-seq to identify immune subpopulations associated with fibrosis, characterize their gene expression profiles, and explore regulatory mechanisms underlying fibrotic remodeling.

What we aim to do

  • Identify pro-fibrotic immune subpopulations
  • Characterize transcriptional signatures at single-cell resolution
  • Investigate regulatory transcription factors and pathways
  • Maintain a reproducible, tutorial-style analysis framework
