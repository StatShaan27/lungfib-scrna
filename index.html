LungFib scRNA-seq

Single-cell RNA sequencing analysis of innate immune cell states in lung fibrosis

Motivation

Chronic lung injury and inflammation can drive fibrosis through aberrant activation of innate immune cells such as macrophages, neutrophils, and dendritic cells. While fibrosis-associated pathways are known at the tissue level, the cell-type–specific transcriptional programs driving pro-fibrotic immune states remain incompletely characterized.

This project uses single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) to resolve immune heterogeneity, identify pro-fibrotic subpopulations, and explore regulatory mechanisms underlying fibrotic remodeling.
