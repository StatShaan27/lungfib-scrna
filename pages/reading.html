Key Papers from the Supervisor

These papers provide direct biological and conceptual grounding for the project, particularly in understanding macrophage-driven fibrotic mechanisms in lung tissue.

  • IL-10 trains macrophage profibrotic function after lung injury
    PubMed: 35107021
    Investigates how IL-10 signaling programs macrophages toward pro-fibrotic functional states following lung injury.
  • Macrophage Cx43 is necessary for fibroblast cytosolic calcium and lung fibrosis after injury
    PubMed: 35634278
    Explores macrophage–fibroblast communication mechanisms underlying fibrotic remodeling.

Books on Lung Biology and Immunity

These texts provide broader biological context for lung inflammation, innate immunity, and pulmonary disease mechanisms.

  • Lung Innate Immunity and Inflammation: Methods and Protocols
    Scott Alper, William Janssen, William J. Janssen
    Focuses on experimental and biological foundations of innate immune responses in the lung.
  • Molecular Basis of Pulmonary Disease: Insights from Rare Disorders
    Ralph J. Panos MD, Francis X. McCormack, Ralph J. Panos
    Provides molecular and genetic perspectives on pulmonary disease mechanisms.
