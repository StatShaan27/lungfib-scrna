What scRNA-seq Measures

Single-cell RNA sequencing measures gene expression at the level of individual cells, producing sparse count matrices influenced by technical noise and biological variability.

Experimental Design and Platforms

Different scRNA-seq platforms vary in throughput, sensitivity, and technical biases. Understanding these trade-offs is essential for downstream analysis.

Strengths and Limitations

scRNA-seq excels at resolving heterogeneity but does not directly measure protein levels, spatial context, or causal regulation.
