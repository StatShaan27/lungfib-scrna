# LungFib scRNA-seq

Single-cell RNA sequencing analysis of innate immune cell states in lung fibrosis, with a focus on transcriptional programs and regulatory factors underlying pro-fibrotic activation.

---

## Motivation

Chronic lung inflammation and injury can lead to fibrosis through persistent activation of innate immune cells such as macrophages, neutrophils, and dendritic cells. While fibrosis-associated pathways are well described at the tissue level, the **cell-type–specific transcriptional programs and regulatory mechanisms** driving pro-fibrotic immune states remain incompletely understood.

Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) enables resolution of heterogeneous immune populations and provides a principled framework to connect cellular states, gene expression programs, and regulatory factors involved in fibrotic remodeling.

---

## Project Goals

- Identify immune cell subpopulations associated with pro-fibrotic activation
- Characterize gene expression signatures at single-cell resolution
- Explore regulatory transcription factors and fibrosis-related pathways
- Maintain a reproducible, tutorial-style analysis framework
